NOTES
=====
* Every link was opened and checked. The YouTube ones were verified through
  the oEmbed endpoint, which fails on a deleted video.
* Arabic coverage is genuinely uneven. Docker and the machine-learning
  fundamentals have excellent Arabic courses. DVC, MLflow, Feast, Ray and
  Terraform effectively have none - where that is true it says so, and points
  at the nearest useful Arabic material instead of padding the list.
* Everything listed is free to watch or read. Some sites ask you to register.
* Image and diagram credits for the slides are in CREDITS.md.
