Session 1 - Leakage-proof pipelines (scikit-learn)
==========================================================================

  Free courses and tutorials
    https://www.kaggle.com/code/alexisbcook/data-leakage
      Data Leakage — Kaggle Learn. START HERE: 20 minutes, runs in your
      browser, nothing to install, and it is about this exact subject
    https://www.kaggle.com/learn/intermediate-machine-learning
      Intermediate Machine Learning — the free Kaggle course the lesson
      above belongs to
    https://carpentries-incubator.github.io/machine-learning-novice-python/08-leakage/index.html
      Data leakage — The Carpentries, from their Python ML lesson (free,
      hands-on)
    https://scikit-learn.org/stable/common_pitfalls.html
      Common pitfalls — scikit-learn. A reference page, not a tutorial:
      read it after one of the above, when you want the exact API rules

  Blogs and articles
    https://machinelearningmastery.com/data-leakage-machine-learning/
      Data Leakage in Machine Learning — Jason Brownlee. The plain-
      English explanation most people learn this from
    https://machinelearningmastery.com/data-preparation-without-data-leakage/
      How to Avoid Data Leakage When Performing Data Preparation — Jason
      Brownlee. This is precisely what this session builds
    https://www.ibm.com/think/topics/data-leakage-machine-learning
      What is Data Leakage in Machine Learning? — IBM. Short, with
      worked examples
    https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leakage_(machine_learning)
      Leakage (machine learning) — Wikipedia. The taxonomy, and where
      the term came from

  Going deeper - the research
    https://arxiv.org/abs/2207.07048
      Leakage and the Reproducibility Crisis in ML-based Science —
      Kapoor & Narayanan (Princeton). Found leakage in 294 published
      papers across 17 disciplines, and gives a taxonomy of eight kinds.
      If you read one thing after this session, this
    https://reproducible.cs.princeton.edu/
      The project behind that paper — the model info sheets and the
      REFORMS checklist, both free to use on your own work

  Video - English
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=n9jz7G68pVg
      What is Data Leakage In Machine Learning? — Krish Naik
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UYHCwBUjxeQ
      What is Data Leakage? Target Leakage and Preprocessing Leakage —
      Six Sigma Pro SMART (the same split this session teaches)

  Video - Arabic
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Ts0vGcc7fuE
      الضبط الناقص والزائد — Underfitting and Overfitting — Hesham Asem
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=8zk0aY39nq0
      شرح بسيط حول K-Fold Cross Validation — أكاديمية الذكاء الإصطناعي
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=KPol_D16gA4
      Cross validation and its types — شرح عربي — Ahmed ibrahim
    https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLtsZ69x5q-X9j44MdSX-NGuOhGXOY0aqH
      تعلم الآلة بالعربي — Elgouhary AI

  ! No Arabic video on leakage itself was found. The three above cover
  ! overfitting and cross-validation, which is the machinery leakage
  ! breaks — do those first, then read the Kaggle lesson in English.

Session 2 - Deep learning (PyTorch, Hugging Face)
==========================================================================

  Free courses and tutorials
    https://course.fast.ai/
      Practical Deep Learning for Coders — fast.ai. START HERE: you
      train a working model in lesson one, then learn why it worked
    https://d2l.ai/
      Dive into Deep Learning — a free book where every chapter is
      runnable PyTorch
    http://neuralnetworksanddeeplearning.com/
      Neural Networks and Deep Learning — Michael Nielsen. Free, and
      builds a network from nothing but arithmetic. The site is http-
      only, so your browser will say 'not secure' — it is a static book,
      there is nothing to type into it
    https://pytorch.org/tutorials/beginner/basics/intro.html
      Learn the Basics — PyTorch official tutorials (free)
    https://huggingface.co/learn/nlp-course
      The Hugging Face NLP Course (free)

  Blogs and articles
    https://jalammar.github.io/illustrated-transformer/
      The Illustrated Transformer — Jay Alammar. The single clearest
      explanation of the architecture behind every model in session 2's
      part 2
    https://colah.github.io/posts/2015-08-Understanding-LSTMs/
      Understanding LSTMs — Chris Olah. Ten years old and still the
      reference
    https://karpathy.github.io/2019/04/25/recipe/
      A Recipe for Training Neural Networks — Andrej Karpathy. What to
      do when your network trains but does not learn
    https://distill.pub/
      Distill — interactive explanations of deep learning ideas, free
      and beautiful

  Going deeper - the research
    https://www.deeplearningbook.org/
      Deep Learning — Goodfellow, Bengio and Courville. The textbook,
      free online. Heavy maths; go here when the intuition is already
      solid

  Video - English
    https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLZHQObOWTQDNU6R1_67000Dx_ZCJB-3pi
      Neural networks — 3Blue1Brown. Watch this before anything else: it
      shows you what a network is doing, geometrically, with no code at
      all
    https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLAqhIrjkxbuWI23v9cThsA9GvCAUhRvKZ
      Neural Networks: Zero to Hero — Andrej Karpathy. Twelve hours,
      from backpropagation by hand to a working GPT, every line typed on
      screen

  Video - Arabic
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=p93xuOXP1jI
      الدورة الكاملة للتعلم العميق — Hesham Asem
    https://www.youtube.com/playlist?list=PLhx4zaYkEjI9kTHTV34iQqRcFoO8RvuxV
      Introduction to Artificial Neural Networks and Deep Learning —
      Hashim EduTech
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=hyPHJI-KGf4
      أساسيات التعلم العميق: كيف تتعلم الشبكة العصبونية — ML Made Simple
    https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=GJ9-u3ySTmQ
      الأساسيات النظرية لتعلم الآلة كاملة في فيديو واحد — Elgouhary AI

  ! Deep learning has real Arabic coverage. Even so, watch the
  ! 3Blue1Brown series above with subtitles — the pictures carry it, and
  ! nothing in Arabic matches them yet.

NOTES
=====
* Every link was opened and checked. The YouTube ones were verified through
  the oEmbed endpoint, which fails on a deleted video.
* Arabic coverage is genuinely uneven. Docker and the machine-learning
  fundamentals have excellent Arabic courses. DVC, MLflow, Feast, Ray and
  Terraform effectively have none - where that is true it says so, and points
  at the nearest useful Arabic material instead of padding the list.
* Everything listed is free to watch or read. Some sites ask you to register.
* Image and diagram credits for the slides are in CREDITS.md.
